\begin{abstract}\noindent
The Time Attraction Law (TAL) factor, $f_L=\exp(-\gamma_L\bar\rho_L)$, with $\gamma_L=GL^2/c^2$, depends on a spherical top-hat density average. Replacing this average with a local midplane-density proxy at all smoothing scales can produce different predictions. We evaluated the spherical average in prescribed three-dimensional baryonic models for 175 SPARC galaxies at 3391 observed rotation-curve radii. The models combine stellar and gas disks with exponential vertical profiles and spherical Hernquist bulges where applicable. The calculation covers 69 smoothing scales, including previously considered Mpc-scale values, with additional refinement near the strongest galaxy-only peaks. The computational reviewer reports a byte-for-byte rerun of the primary calculation (stated by the reviewer, not independently verified here) and supplied 13 selected mass integrals recomputed with its own integration code; these agree with the archived masses to within $2.96\times10^{-6}$. The largest galaxy-attributable $|1-f_L|$ is approximately $2.30\times10^{-7}$, occurring in UGC03546 at $R=0.63$\,kpc and $L\approx0.825$\,kpc. At the previously fitted $L=2.3714$\,Mpc, the maximum exponent $\gamma_L\bar\rho_L$ is $1.37\times10^{-9}$ for the galaxy alone and $1.21\times10^{-8}$ after adding a uniform matter background. Under the explicitly specified forward velocity rule, $V_\mathrm{pred}^2=f_L V_\mathrm{bar}^2$, the median reduced $\chi^2$ on the fixed 123-galaxy subset returns to approximately 3.1433, rather than the proxy-based value of 2.7083. Thus the earlier fitted-scale improvement does not survive the specified top-hat averaging. These results are conditional on the adopted baryonic profiles and velocity coupling; they are not a general exclusion of every possible TAL dynamics or an experimental detection. The deposit provides the existing code, inputs, numerical outputs, sensitivity summaries, and computational review materials.
\end{abstract}

\section{Definitions}
Spherical top-hat average around the evaluation point $\mathbf{x}$ (\texttt{Time\_Attraction.pdf}, Def.~1, Eq.~1), scale factor (Def.~2, Eq.~2) and TAL factor (Def.~3, Eq.~7):
\begin{equation*}
\bar\rho_L(\mathbf{x})=\frac{3}{4\pi L^3}\int_{|\mathbf{x}'-\mathbf{x}|\le L}\rho(\mathbf{x}')\,\mathrm{d}^3x',\qquad
\gamma_L=\frac{GL^2}{c^2},\qquad f_L=\exp(-\gamma_L\bar\rho_L).
\end{equation*}
The reported deviation is $|1-f_L|=-\mathrm{expm1}(-\gamma_L\bar\rho_L)$. The ratio $\bar\rho_L/\rho_\mathrm{mid}$ is reported only as a diagnostic of the seventh-model proxy; it is not the TAL $f_L$. Constants: $G=6.67430\times10^{-11}$\,m$^3$\,kg$^{-1}$\,s$^{-2}$, $c=299\,792\,458$\,m\,s$^{-1}$, $\Msun=1.98847\times10^{30}$\,kg, 1\,pc $=3.085677581\times10^{16}$\,m.

\section{Ball-mass integrals as implemented}
\subsection{Disk components}
For a ball of radius $L$ centred at $(R_0,0,0)$, each disk component contributes
\begin{equation*}
M_\mathrm{disk}(R_0,L)=\int_{R_\mathrm{min}}^{R_\mathrm{max}}R\,\Sigma(R)\,I(R)\,\mathrm{d}R,\qquad R_\mathrm{min}=\max(0,R_0-L),\; R_\mathrm{max}=\min(R_0+L,R_\mathrm{cut}).
\end{equation*}
Using $z\to-z$ symmetry and the vertical substitution $u=1-e^{-z/h}$, for which $\tfrac{\Sigma}{2h}e^{-z/h}\mathrm{d}z=\tfrac{\Sigma}{2}\mathrm{d}u$,
\begin{equation*}
I(R)=2\pi u_\mathrm{full}+\int_{u_\mathrm{full}}^{u_\mathrm{max}}\Delta\phi\bigl(R,z(u)\bigr)\,\mathrm{d}u,\qquad z(u)=-h\ln(1-u),
\end{equation*}
\begin{equation*}
\Delta\phi=2\arccos\Bigl(\mathrm{clip}\Bigl[\tfrac{R^2+R_0^2+z^2-L^2}{2RR_0},-1,1\Bigr]\Bigr),
\end{equation*}
with $u_\mathrm{max}=1-e^{-z_\mathrm{max}/h}$, $z_\mathrm{max}=\sqrt{L^2-(R-R_0)^2}$, and $u_\mathrm{full}=1-e^{-z_\mathrm{full}/h}$, $z_\mathrm{full}=\sqrt{L^2-(R+R_0)^2}$ when positive (otherwise $u_\mathrm{full}=0$). Below $z_\mathrm{full}$ the whole ring lies inside the ball, giving the analytic full-ring part $2\pi u_\mathrm{full}$. The square-root edge behaviour at $u_\mathrm{max}$ is removed by $u=u_\mathrm{max}-(u_\mathrm{max}-u_\mathrm{full})t^2$, $\mathrm{d}u=2(u_\mathrm{max}-u_\mathrm{full})\,t\,\mathrm{d}t$, $t\in[0,1]$.

\textbf{Quadrature.} In $R$, breakpoints are placed at $R_\mathrm{min}$, $R_\mathrm{max}$, $|L-R_0|$, $R_0$, all tabulated radii and $R_\mathrm{last}+kR_d$ ($k=1\dots15$) for stars or $kR_g$ ($k=1\dots25$) for gas. Each sub-interval is split to a width $\le\max(\min(L/4,s/2),10^{-4}\,\mathrm{kpc})$ ($s=R_d$ or $R_g$), with $n_R=16$ Gauss--Legendre nodes per sub-interval. In $t$, $n_T=32$ Gauss--Legendre nodes are used.

\subsection{Bulge}
With $d=R_0$, the fraction of the shell of radius $r$ inside the ball is $F(r)=\mathrm{clip}\bigl[(L^2-(r-d)^2)/(4rd),0,1\bigr]$, so
\begin{equation*}
M_\mathrm{bulge}(d,L)=M_b(<L-d)\,[L>d]+\int_{|L-d|}^{L+d}4\pi r^2\rho_b(r)\,F(r)\,\mathrm{d}r,
\end{equation*}
evaluated on geometric sub-intervals (9 log-spaced points from $\max(|L-d|,10^{-6}(L+d))$ to $L+d$, resolving the $1/r$ cusp) with $n_B=64$ Gauss--Legendre nodes each.

\subsection{Whole-galaxy shortcut and total}
If $L\ge R_0+\max(R_\mathrm{cut,disk},R_\mathrm{cut,gas})+60\max(h_\star,h_\mathrm{gas})$, the total model disk mass and $M_\mathrm{gas}$ are used directly; the bulge is still integrated. Finally $\bar\rho_L=(M_\star+M_\mathrm{gas}+M_\mathrm{bulge})/(4\pi L^3/3)$, with $1\,\Msun\,\mathrm{kpc}^{-3}=6.768\times10^{-29}$\,kg\,m$^{-3}$.

\section{Scope and limitations}
\textbf{Applies to:} the prescribed baryonic SPARC models above; constant disk scale height $h_z$; a mass-normalised exponential gas disk (not the observed $\Sigma_\mathrm{HI}(R)$); a spherical Hernquist bulge; the forward rule $V_\mathrm{pred}^2=f_LV_\mathrm{bar}^2$; and a static uniform background.

\textbf{Does not apply to:} dark-matter haloes (by the scaling $\gamma_L\bar\rho_L\sim|\Phi|/c^2$, bounded at about $V^2/c^2\approx10^{-6}$); groups, clusters and environments (about $10^{-5}$ for a rich cluster; both order-of-magnitude estimates from the package README); other TAL velocity couplings; cosmological dynamics; and any experimental detection or measured sensitivity.
