## 3. Numerically Integrated FLRW Background Models vs. Fixed Phase-Shift and Literature Peak-Height Inputs

### 3.1. Equations Actually Integrated by `cmb_and_cosmology_audit.py`
For a given parameter set $(\Omega_b, \Omega_c, \Omega_\gamma, \Omega_\nu, \Omega_{\Lambda,\text{eff}}, \Omega_K)$, `cmb_and_cosmology_audit.py` integrates the Friedmann equation:
$$E^2(z) \;\equiv\; \frac{H^2(z)}{H_0^2} \;=\; \Omega_{r,0}(1+z)^4 + \Omega_{m,0}(1+z)^3 + \Omega_K(1+z)^2 + \Omega_{\Lambda,\text{eff}}, \qquad q_0 \;=\; \frac{\Omega_{m,0}}{2} + \Omega_{r,0} - \Omega_{\Lambda,\text{eff}}.$$
The baryon–photon sound speed and the comoving sound horizon at recombination ($z_* = 1089.80$) are integrated via:
$$c_s(z) \;=\; \frac{c}{\sqrt{3\bigl(1 + R_b(z)\bigr)}}, \qquad R_b(z) \;=\; \frac{3\Omega_b}{4\Omega_\gamma(1+z)}, \qquad r_s(z_*) \;=\; \int_{z_*}^\infty \frac{c_s(z)\,dz}{H_0 E(z)}.$$
The comoving line-of-sight distance, curvature-corrected comoving angular distance, and acoustic scale are:
$$\chi(z_*) \;=\; \frac{c}{H_0}\int_0^{z_*} \frac{dz}{E(z)}, \qquad D_M(z_*) \;=\; \begin{cases} \dfrac{c}{H_0\sqrt{\Omega_K}}\sinh\!\left(\dfrac{\sqrt{\Omega_K}H_0}{c}\chi(z_*)\right) & (\Omega_K > 0), \\[8pt] \chi(z_*) & (\Omega_K = 0), \end{cases} \qquad \theta_* \;=\; \frac{r_s(z_*)}{D_M(z_*)}, \qquad \ell_A \;=\; \frac{\pi}{\theta_*}.$$

## 4. Theoretical Assessment of Negative $\rho_\Lambda = -5.96\times 10^{-27}\;\text{kg/m}^3$ and Positive $\rho_{\text{clock,cosmo}}$ Compensation (Review Point 3)

1. **Why the canonical scalar energy density is positive in the minimal action of Steps 22–23:**
   - In the Einstein-frame action specified in Step 23 (`step23_gw170817_and_lensing_en.md`, Section 2.1), the scalar potential is $V(\phi_L) = +\frac{1}{2}m_\phi^2\phi_L^2 \ge 0$. The time–time component of the standard variational stress–energy tensor $T_{\mu\nu}^{(\phi)} = \frac{c^4}{8\pi G\tilde{\alpha}_L}\bigl[\nabla_\mu\phi_L\nabla_\nu\phi_L - \frac{1}{2}g_{\mu\nu}((\nabla\phi_L)^2 + L^{-2}\phi_L^2)\bigr]$ for a spatially homogeneous field ($\nabla_i\phi_L = 0$) is:
     $$\rho_{\phi,\text{can}} \;=\; \frac{1}{c^2}T_{00}^{(\phi)} \;=\; \frac{c^2}{8\pi G\tilde{\alpha}_L}\left[\frac{\dot{\phi}_L^2}{2c^2} + \frac{\phi_L^2}{2L^2}\right] \;\ge\; 0.$$
   - Consequently, the minimal canonical action of Steps 22–23 does **not** generate a negative vacuum energy density; the sign $\rho_\Lambda = -V(\phi_{\text{cosmo}})/c^2 = -5.96\times 10^{-27}\;\text{kg/m}^3$ can only be introduced as a hypothesis belonging to a separate macroscopic/cosmological branch.
2. **Consequences of an uncompensated negative $\rho_\Lambda = -5.96\times 10^{-27}\;\text{kg/m}^3$ ($\Omega_\Lambda = -0.685101$) on a separate branch:**
   - **In flat space ($\Omega_K = 0$, `TAL_uncompensated_negative_rhoLambda_flat`):** today one would have $E^2(z=0) = \Omega_{r,0} + \Omega_{m,0} + \Omega_\Lambda = -0.370202 < 0$, meaning the universe would have halted and recollapsed at $a_{\max} = (\Omega_{m,0}/|\Omega_\Lambda|)^{1/3} = \mathbf{0.771762}$ ($z_{\text{turn}} = \mathbf{0.295737}$), never reaching $a = 1$.
   - **With spatial curvature closure ($\Omega_K = 1 - \Omega_{m,0} - \Omega_\Lambda = +1.370202$, `TAL_uncompensated_negative_rhoLambda`):** the universe reaches $a = 1$ (and turns around in the future at $a_{\max} = 1.517519$), but strongly decelerates today ($q_0 = \mathbf{+0.842603}$, vs. the observed accelerating $q_0 \approx -0.53$), and the large positive curvature stretches the comoving angular distance to $D_M(z_*) = 34\,916.42\;\text{Mpc}$, shifting the acoustic scale to $100\,\theta_* = \mathbf{0.413462}$ ($\ell_A = 759.83$, $\ell_1^{\text{est}} \approx 553.96$).
