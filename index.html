CORRIGENDUM v8.2 (2026-10-04):

In v8.1 reported R=(r+1)/(r+2). Correct per Bellini & Sawicki (2014) eq 2.18 is R=(1+r)/(1+2r) where r=α_M/α_B. Inversion: r=(1-R)/(2R-1). Both give R=2/3 at r=1, but differ at r≠1. Acknowledgment to Jordi Audet Palau for pointing out the correction.
